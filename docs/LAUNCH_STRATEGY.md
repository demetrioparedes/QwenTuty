# LAUNCH & POST-LAUNCH STRATEGY

## 1. Phase 1: Alpha (Internal)
- **Duration:** 1 Week.
- **Audience:** Internal team + family members.
- **Goal:** Catch game-breaking bugs and validate core fun factor.
- **Action:** Manual testing of all 12 missions.

## 2. Phase 2: Beta (Closed)
- **Duration:** 2 Weeks.
- **Audience:** 20 Kids (12-13 years old).
- **Goal:** UX validation and difficulty balancing.
- **Action:**
    - Distribute via private link.
    - Collect telemetry (completion rates, time stuck).
    - Post-game survey (Google Forms).

## 3. Phase 3: Public Launch (MVP)
- **Scope:** Missions 1-6 (Levels 1 & 2).
- **Goal:** Initial user acquisition and course integration.
- **Channels:**
    - Email newsletter to existing students.
    - Social media (Twitter/LinkedIn) targeting parents/educators.
    - Integration into the main Antigravity dashboard.

## 4. Phase 4: Full Release & Updates
- **Scope:** All 12 Missions + Certification.
- **Features:** Leaderboards (opt-in), Social Sharing.
- **Retention:**
    - "Daily Challenge" mode.
    - New "Master Class" (Level 5) as DLC.

## 5. Metrics to Watch
- **Completion Rate:** Target > 60% for Level 1.
- **Time per Mission:** Should match estimates in GDD.
- **Churn Point:** Identify where most users drop off.
